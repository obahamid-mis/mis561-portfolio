In Flex 4, I calculated each account’s net contribution by subtracting its tier-based cost to serve from its total profit in Excel’s “Pivot Table - Account Summary” sheet, then summed those values by discount band in Tableau. In Power BI, I would build total net contribution as a measure that subtracts the selected accounts’ total service costs from their total profit. A measure fits because the result needs to change when Marcus filters by service tier or customer discount band. Having this calculation in the model would let Marcus compare profitability across those groups himself during a meeting, without asking me to build another view..
